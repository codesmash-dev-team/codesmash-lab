Privacy Policy

Your health data stays in the Health app

Last updated 2 October 2026

CodeSmash Lab is an iPhone game app that gets you moving with data from the Health app. It has no user accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. Health data and play history never leave your device.

What the app reads from Health

With your permission, the app reads the following to show missions, figures and stats on the Health tab.

  • Steps
  • Walking and running distance
  • Flights climbed
  • Exercise time
  • Sleep
  • Heart rate
  • Resting heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Walking heart rate average
  • Walking steadiness
  • Workouts and workout routes

The app asks Health to let it know when your step count changes, even while the app is closed, so the day's missions stay current. What it reads is only shown on screen. The app keeps no copy of its own and sends nothing off the device.

What the app writes to Health

When you finish a walk you started in the app, the app saves it to Health as a workout with its route and its check result. The distance is saved only for walks that pass the check. All of this happens only if you have allowed the app to write. The app never edits or deletes anything else in Health, and you can view and delete saved walks in the Health app.

Location and motion

The app uses your iPhone's location and step counting only during a walk you start yourself, including while the screen is locked or you switch to another app during that walk, to check that it is a real walk. The app only asks for "While Using the App" location access and does not use your location when you are not walking. A walk in progress is not written to the device.

What is stored on your device

  • Play history: which games you played, how many times, for how long, and when you last played
  • Achievements you have unlocked
  • The app's notification inbox and your notification settings
  • The profile photo or character you picked
  • The latest game list and cover images the app loaded

This data stays in the app's storage on your iPhone and is included in your device backups (iCloud or a computer) according to your device settings. It is never sent to us.

Internet connections

When you open the app, it loads the game list, descriptions and cover images from CODESMASH's Game Center server (center.game.gamification.in.th). These requests carry no health data, no play history and no identifier for you or your device. The server only sees the ordinary connection details every internet request has, such as your IP address.

Each game is a web page loaded from the internet and opened inside the app. The app sends no health data or play history to the games. Whoever hosts a game's web page sees the same ordinary connection details, and a game's web page may remember your progress in the game on your device, as websites normally do.

Some games have a CODESMASH Game Center leaderboard and ask you, before you play, to choose between playing as a Guest and signing in with Google. As a Guest you don't sign up: the Game Center server creates a guest account and keeps your scores with it, and the leaderboard shows your score next to the word Guest. If you sign in with Google, the leaderboard shows the name on your Google account instead. Inside the app, only Guest play is available for now. Some games let you play a friend online, which sends your moves through the game's server while you play. None of this includes health data or play history from the app.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Health to read and write the items listed above
  • Location (While Using the App) during a walk you start yourself
  • Motion & Fitness to count steps during a walk
  • Notifications created by the app on your device, for example a reminder at 20:00 on days you have not reached your goal, or when you unlock an achievement. No server sends notifications.
  • Photos to pick a photo from your library as your profile picture. The photo you pick is copied into the app's storage and is never uploaded.

Apple Watch and Live Activity

The CodeSmash Lab app on Apple Watch starts, pauses and ends walks from your wrist, talking directly to your paired iPhone. During a walk, progress appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island through a Live Activity, which is updated on the device and does not go through a server.

Deleting and controlling your data

  • Turn off location, Motion & Fitness, notification and photo access at any time in the Settings app, under CodeSmash Lab.
  • Manage Health access and the walks the app has saved in the Health app: tap your profile picture, then look for the section about apps.
  • Delete the app to remove everything in its storage. Walks already saved to Health stay in Health until you delete them there.
  • Scores on a game's leaderboard are kept by Game Center and stay when you delete the app. To have them removed, email the address under Provider and contact below with the game, the score and the day you played.

This website

This website is made of static pages with no scripts, no forms and no counters, and it loads nothing from other websites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical details of each request, such as your IP address, under GitHub's own policy.
